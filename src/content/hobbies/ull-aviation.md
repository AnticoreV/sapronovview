Ultralight (ULL) flying is the closest thing to the way flying was always meant to feel: small aircraft, short grass strips, and enough headroom in the rules to actually enjoy the sky.

I got into it because I've always liked learning something new and discovering things. I train at the Letňany Aero Club in Prague and have 15 hours in my logbook as a student pilot.

What I like most is the discipline. Checklists, weather briefings, weight and balance: flying rewards the same habits that make good software, preparation, redundancy and never trusting a single source of truth.
