Sport is the non-negotiable part of my week. It's where the thinking stops and the body takes over, and I come back to problems sharper than when I left.

Most weeks that means the gym, three or four times. In winter I snowboard, and I like hiking and exploring new places. I've also tried padel, rock climbing and via ferrata. The two things I'm proudest of: a parachute jump, and finishing a D-grade via ferrata.
