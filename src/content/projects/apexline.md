## Overview

Apexline is a peer-to-peer motorcycle rental marketplace for Europe. Riders book bikes from local owners; owners earn on the days their bike would otherwise sit idle.

## Stage

Research and validation. I am testing demand before building the product: market research and a landing page in Czech and English. There is no product and no traction to report yet.
